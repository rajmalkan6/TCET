Essential Gardening Tips

  • Start with quality soil for a healthy foundation for your plants.
  • Water your garden in the morning to reduce evaporation.
  • Use mulch to retain moisture and suppress weeds.
  • Prune plants regularly to encourage growth.
  • Ensure your plants receive the right amount of sunlight.
